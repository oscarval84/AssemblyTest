ACME INC. — MASTER SUPPLIER AGREEMENT
Template version {{templateVersion}}

This Master Supplier Agreement is entered into between Acme Inc. ("Acme"), a managed
service provider, and {{companyLegalName}} ("Supplier").

1. SCOPE
Supplier may be engaged to provide contingent staffing services to Acme's client
programs. Each engagement is governed by this agreement together with the terms of
the specific program under which the placement is made.

2. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR RELATIONSHIP
Supplier is an independent contractor. Nothing in this agreement creates an
employment, partnership or joint venture relationship between the parties, and
Supplier is solely responsible for its own personnel.

3. INSURANCE
Supplier shall maintain, at its own expense, the coverage required by each program
into which it is enrolled, and shall provide a current certificate of insurance
naming Acme as an additional insured. Supplier shall notify Acme in writing before
any lapse, cancellation or material reduction in coverage.

4. WORKER ELIGIBILITY AND SCREENING
Supplier warrants that every worker it places is legally authorised to work in the
United States and has been screened to the standard required by the applicable
program.

5. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party shall protect the other's confidential information with no less care
than it applies to its own, and shall use it only to perform this agreement. This
obligation survives termination.

6. DATA PROTECTION
Supplier shall process personal information received under this agreement only as
necessary to provide the services, and shall notify Acme without undue delay of any
unauthorised access to that information.

7. INVOICING AND PAYMENT
Supplier shall invoice through the process specified by the applicable program.
Acme shall pay undisputed amounts within the terms stated in that program.

8. RECORDS AND AUDIT
Supplier shall retain records substantiating its performance and its compliance
with this agreement, and shall make them available to Acme on reasonable notice.
Acme's own clients may audit these records through Acme.

9. COMPLIANCE WITH LAW
Supplier shall comply with all applicable federal, state and local laws, including
wage and hour, anti-discrimination and worker classification requirements.

10. TERM AND TERMINATION
This agreement begins on the date of signature below and continues until terminated
by either party on thirty (30) days' written notice. Obligations relating to active
placements, confidentiality and records survive termination.

11. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This agreement, together with the program terms it references, is the entire
agreement between the parties on this subject and supersedes prior discussions.

12. ELECTRONIC SIGNATURE
The parties agree that this agreement may be executed electronically, and that a
typed name submitted through Acme's supplier onboarding platform, together with the
recorded identity, timestamp and originating address of the signer, constitutes a
binding signature.
